Privacy Policy

Kabul Property Hub collects only the information necessary to list and display properties. This includes contact details, property descriptions, and photos submitted by users.

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Your contact information is only used to facilitate property inquiries via WhatsApp or email.

Photos uploaded become part of the public listing. Do not upload images containing sensitive personal information.

We use local browser storage for saved properties and language preferences. No tracking cookies are used.

For privacy questions, contact kabulpropertyhub@gmail.com.
